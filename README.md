## About me

I am an AI/ML Engineer with 4+ years of experience at JPMorgan Chase, working across production machine learning and Generative AI systems.

My work has covered fraud risk modeling, large-scale feature engineering, computer vision, graph-derived features, retrieval-augmented generation, agentic workflows, model evaluation, and production ML systems.

> **The part I care about most comes after "the model works": How do we evaluate it, make it reliable, observe it, scale it, and know when it fails?**

## How I like to build

- **Measure before claiming:** benchmarks and evaluations over adjectives.
- **Make failures observable:** traces, metrics, and failure cases are product features.
- **Use deterministic control where it helps:** reserve agentic reasoning for places where it adds value.
- **Design for real constraints:** latency, cost, safety, maintainability, and scale matter.
